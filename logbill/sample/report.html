How the numbers were made

Prices, assumptions and notes
  • Prices: AWS Price List for CloudWatch, published 2026-10-07, read 2026-10-27. Edit prices.json to change them.
  • Ingestion: AWS/Logs IncomingBytes per log group over 30 days, scaled to 30.
  • Storage: storedBytes from DescribeLogGroups at the region's GB-month rate. Retention savings assume the stored bytes built up evenly since the group was created.
  • Per-line cost: Logs Insights pattern counts over a short window, weighted by line length, applied to the group's monthly ingestion cost.
  • Vended logs (Lambda, /aws/vendedlogs/) are priced on the region's volume tiers.
